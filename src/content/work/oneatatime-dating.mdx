OneAtATime Dating is an introverted-styled iOS mobile dating app. Instead of optimizing for as many swipes and matches as possible, the experience focuses on getting to know someone more in depth.

## Problem

There isn't a "dating app for introverts." Traditional dating apps like Tinder, Bumble and Hinge drain an introvert's social battery. Keeping up with multiple conversations, not getting to know someone or their true intentions and guessing if someone actually likes you is a lot to deal with.

## Design Iteration

I explored multiple Figma prototypes before building the product, including profile card layouts, expanded profile views, match states, and different ways to reveal more information over time. I also iterated on the chat timer, testing different placements and visual treatments so the 24-hour timer wouldn't feel overwhelming, but would still ensure the user could gauge another user's interest depending on if they let 24 hours go by or not.

## Solution

I designed a one-at-a-time matching flow where users discover more about other users by revealing more profile information, prompts, and photos. This way, users are incentivized to get to know a potential match more in depth, aren't overwhelmed with multiple matches, and can focus on a more enjoyable dating experience.

## Key Product Decisions

- Replaced traditional dating app "Likes" with "Reveals" so users are more incentivized to get to know someone on a deeper level.
- Chatting with matches reveals more profile information, prompts, and photos, which keeps the conversation going and encourages users to have more in-depth conversations.
- Added an "interest timer," so users can accurately gauge if someone's actually interested in them or not.
- Created an ocean-inspired visual identity to reflect getting to know someone more in-depth and on a deeper level, while keeping things calm.
- Refined the landing page to better explain the product's differentiation and show the app experience more clearly.
- Reduced the amount of sign up questions, increasing the amount of active users and reducing incomplete sign ups.
- Users can access the dating page and are asked to complete a few more questions before they can match with another user, increasing the amount of time spent on the app and incentivizing users to return.
