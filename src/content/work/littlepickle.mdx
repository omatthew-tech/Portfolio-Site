## The Problem

Deciding "who plays next" can be a challenge. Even if you've tried the "paddle system", that still doesn't account for a player's skill level and can lead to unbalanced match making. LittlePickle attempts to solve this through an advanced queue system, balanced match making and a little competitiveness to keep things fun
